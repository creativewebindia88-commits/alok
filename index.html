About Us

OPHLSSA is a non-profit organization based in Gorakhpur, India. We are dedicated to holistic social upliftment through education, healthcare, skill development, and environmental initiatives.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged sections of society with quality education, healthcare and livelihood opportunities.

Our Vision

A just and equitable society where every individual has the opportunity to thrive and contribute.

Our Services

Education Support

Scholarships, study material, and after-school programs for children in need.

Health Camps

Free medical check-ups, awareness drives, and distribution of medicines.

Skill Development

Vocational training for youth and women to enhance employability.
